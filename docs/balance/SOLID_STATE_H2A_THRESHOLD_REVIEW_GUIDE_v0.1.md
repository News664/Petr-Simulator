# SOLID STATE — H2A Threshold Review Guide
## Version 0.1

This guide is for interpreting the calibration after Claude reports it. It is not
a scoring algorithm.

## Avoid one-dimensional selection

Do not choose the arm with the highest completion rate by default.

A plausible provisional H2A profile should improve the ending economy without:
- making most lives terminate immediately after commitment;
- returning faction endings to Phase 1.2 dominance;
- creating severe material-family determinism;
- collapsing most endings into one age bucket;
- pushing visible-stat behavior into clearly pathological extremes.

## Priority order for review

1. **Completion is usable**
   - Current H2A is too nonterminal.
   - Roughly 45–70% is a useful provisional human-playtest zone, not a final
     target.

2. **25–34 is restored**
   - It should become a major/largest bucket.
   - This is more important in this threshold pass than forcing the final 18–24
     target.

3. **Commitment timing makes sense**
   - Prefer late 20s / early 30s over mean mid/late 30s.
   - Avoid a profile where commitment becomes almost universal immediately at 18.

4. **65+ survivor pileup shrinks**
   - Do not select a profile that still leaves most completed endings at 65+.

5. **Old core routes reappear**
   - Corporate, Museum, Academic, Legal, Finance should become observable again.

6. **Faction terminality stays secondary**
   - Factions may be common world actors; direct faction endings should not
     dominate the whole game.

7. **Material guardrails remain plausible**
   - Do not trade completion for de facto material determinism.

8. **Stat telemetry informs the next content patch**
   - High CHR/INT tails are not automatically a reason to clamp.
   - Low MNY/SPR are not automatically a reason to buff.
   - Use data to design authored pressure and high-stat attention events later.

## If no arm is good

Do not average LOW/MID/HIGH or invent a midpoint automatically.

Instead report which dimension each arm fails and design the next move explicitly.
