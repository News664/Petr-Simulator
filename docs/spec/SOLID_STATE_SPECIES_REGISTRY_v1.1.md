## Notes

### Human (`HUMAN`)
Baseline morphology and institutional default. Human has one extra free allocation point rather than fixed stat modifiers.

### Elf (`ELF`)
Wood outcomes should be polished, arboreal, carved, lacquered, sculptural, or otherwise refined; wood is not automatically rustic or cheap.

### Dwarf (`DWARF`)
Durable/industrial material associations; Human-scale infrastructure and bureaucracy are recurring social frictions.

### Winged Kin (`WINGED_KIN`)
Angel/天使 is everyday shorthand and scientifically inaccurate. Wings vary in color/functionality; accommodation and symbolic objectification are major event hooks.

### Demonkin (`DEMONKIN`)
Horns/tails and related traits are not moral indicators. Bureaucratic danger assumptions and ceremonial symbolism provide event hooks.

### Dragonkin (`DRAGONKIN`)
Human-sized draconic humanoids. Precious-material valuation, collateral, insurance, and social expectations are recurring hooks.
