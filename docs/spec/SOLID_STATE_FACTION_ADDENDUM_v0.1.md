# SOLID STATE — Faction Addendum
## Version 0.1 — Phase 1.2 initial layer

Factions enter the first-playable design as a **lightweight authored context**, not a new reputation simulator.

Core rules:

- Faction state uses discrete flags only.
- No numeric loyalty, reputation, morality, or alignment meter.
- The player still makes no mid-life faction choice; affiliation/contact emerges through automatic events.
- `*_CONTACT` means the protagonist has crossed paths with the organization. It does **not** mean membership or ideological agreement.
- Factions may generate later obligations, follow-ups, special events and route intersections.
- Faction context does not automatically select a material family.
- Late-school faction exposure is permitted from age 16 where age-appropriate, but canonical Permanent Form endings remain age 18+.
- The previously discussed DnD/alignment-inspired faction expansion remains deferred; this is not that system.

Initial factions:

1. **Department of Mobility and Material Status (DMMS)** — government classification, compliance and administrative failure.
2. **Everlasting Mutual** — insurance, claims and future-form financial incentives.
3. **Meridian Preservation Group** — consumer preservation and permanence-as-product.
4. **Continuity Research Institute (CRI)** — paid studies, transformation exposure and temporal/continuity research.
5. **Black Ledger Cooperative** — underground brokerage of future-form rights and questionable collateral.
6. **Order of the Last Posture** — public ceremonial/stillness organization with a cultic permanence trajectory.

Faction events should feel like ordinary institutions becoming consequential, not like a separate faction-selection game.
