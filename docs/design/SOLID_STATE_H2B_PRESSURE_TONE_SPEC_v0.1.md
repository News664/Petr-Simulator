# SOLID STATE — H2B Pressure & Tone Design Spec
## Version 0.1

## Frozen direction

LOW is the provisional design/simulation baseline for this iteration, not yet a canonical threshold freeze. The purpose of Batch 008 is to move **why and when lives become dangerous**, not merely lower every gate again.

### Natural attribute curves

Without unusual outside intervention:

| Stat | Young | Middle | Old |
|---|---|---|---|
| STR | clear growth | stable / minor change | clear decline |
| CHR | slight growth possible | broadly stable | broadly stable / modest decline |
| INT | clear growth | slight growth / stable | stable / slight decline |
| MNY | variable | variable | variable |
| SPR | variable | variable | variable |

These are content-authoring curves, **not passive annual drift**. Events still explain every change.

### Stat-value philosophy

- High stats are usually good. Growth must remain meaningful.
- Rare high-stat adverse outcomes are surprise exceptions with additional prerequisites, not a symmetrical pressure system.
- Low stats increasingly open less-favorable outcomes and faster paths toward Permanent Form.
- Low stats do not trigger generic death. Institutions exploit or “solve” the protagonist's situation.
- Avoid quick collapse: normal adverse events usually move a visible stat by -1/-2, rarely -3.
- Rare recovery from a bad spiral is necessary. Some recoveries are clean luck. Others are bargains.

### Recovery / FIX magnitude

Under provisional LOW, standard commitment is around FIX 28. Therefore a Faustian +3/+4/+5 recovery must not carry cosmetic FIX.

- clean luck: FIX +0 to +2
- costly but legitimate recovery: FIX +0 to +6 plus money/other-stat cost
- predatory institutional recovery: normally FIX +10 to +14
- exceptional especially powerful bargains may test +15/+16 only if the first Batch 008 diagnostic still leaves commitment late

Do not automatically attach a hidden cost to every good event. Predictable “good news is always fake” weakens the humor.

## Basic Continuity Insurance

All citizens have a basic continuity benefit. It provides the graceful low-STR answer to implausible century-long survival at STR 0/-5.

1. STR <=1 can open a Functional Continuity Review.
2. Two years later: recovery closes it; wealth/intelligence/Second Opinion can buy a strong mobility-preserving rescue; persistent low STR receives covered stabilization with a large FIX increase.
3. STR <=-3 expedites the decision.
4. If critically weak after review, the insurer can approve permanent stabilization. Existing MAT is preserved; uncommitted standard public coverage may use SYNT.

Tone: the permanent ending is presented as successful insurance coverage, not painful illness. “The approval does not cover continued mobility.”

## Low CHR writing rule

CHR is social attractiveness/access, not a narrator's objective beauty score. Low CHR can arise from behavior, presentation, isolation, prejudice, reputation or species stereotyping. Institutions may use insulting euphemisms; the narration must not simply call the protagonist ugly.

## Housing

Housing stays common. It should feel like a real social system, not a repeated relocation button. Batch 008 adds employer accommodation, permanent roommates, species retrofits, manifestation damage claims, insurance-compliant units and a structural-housing route.

Only some housing events move home. Existing moving event EVT-ORD-HOU-0002 becomes once-per-run.

The structural-housing route is deliberately tempting for low MNY: major rent relief can add +12 FIX, then Stone/Metal evidence may lead to an architectural commitment and ending. Ambiguous multiple manifestations must not be resolved by arbitrary variant order.

## Factions

- OPTED_OUT is terminal unless a future explicitly authored talent/anomaly states otherwise.
- CLOSED is terminal.
- At most one faction may be personally active (CONTACTED/ENGAGED/COMMITTED) at once.
- Other factions may still appear in news/lore/world events.
- A later different faction may contact after the active chain reaches OPTED_OUT/CLOSED.
- Do not show faction labels/progress in normal UI. Continuity comes from prose callbacks.
- Insert one new personalized middle event between disposition and existing escalation for all six factions.

## All-female world

All sapient humanoid characters in canonical v1 prose are female. Use female or gender-neutral references. This is a content invariant, not a chat-memory preference.

## Preschool age semantics

Age 0: birth/registration/insurance/family/medical/home preparation; no meaningful protagonist agency.
Age 1: guardian-mediated home/development events.
Age 2–3: simple child behavior/family circumstances.
Age 4–5: preschool/social agency becomes plausible.

Batch 008 includes prose-only fixes for two early household events; the integration run must audit every event eligible at age 0 or 1.
