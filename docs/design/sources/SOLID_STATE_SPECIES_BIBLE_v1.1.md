# SOLID STATE — Species Bible
## Version 1.1 — Refreshed and Current

## 1. Purpose

Species in Solid State shape morphology, social experience, and soft material tendencies without creating separate civilizations or deterministic transformation destinies.

All six starting species share the same broadly modern Human-driven society: the same cities, schools, currencies, legal systems, employers, hospitals, museums, transit, insurers, universities, and media.

The setting's institutions were historically designed around Humans. Non-Human characters therefore encounter accommodation problems, stereotypes, valuation assumptions, and bureaucratic mismatches rather than belonging to isolated fantasy nations.

## 2. Core gameplay rule

Species is rolled before talent selection.

Starting order:

1. receive one random species;
2. receive ten random talents;
3. choose exactly three talents;
4. allocate starting visible attributes;
5. life simulation begins at age 0.

Species is permanent biological identity for the run.

`registeredSpecies` may differ only through explicitly authored effects such as `Wrong Species Certificate`.

## 3. Starting species

| Species | zh-TW | Starting rule |
|---|---|---|
| Human | 人類 | 21 free allocation points; no fixed stat modifier |
| Elf | 精靈 | CHR +1, INT +1, STR -1 |
| Dwarf | 矮人 | STR +2, CHR -1 |
| Winged Kin | 翼族 | CHR +1, SPR +1, STR -1 |
| Demonkin | 魔裔 | CHR +2, SPR -1 |
| Dragonkin | 龍人族 | STR +1, MNY +1, SPR -1 |

All non-Humans receive the normal 20 free allocation points before fixed species modifiers.

## 4. Species and material tendencies

Species tendencies act mainly at the **Transformation family-selection layer** before Material Commitment.

They are qualitative:
- Primary
- Secondary
- Neutral
- Uncommon

They are not guarantees.

A childhood hint, species Primary tendency, or first manifestation is still soft evidence. Only an authored event setting Material Commitment creates the ordinary material hard lock.

Exact numeric multipliers are intentionally absent from this Bible and must be simulation-tested.

## 5. Human

Human is the institutional baseline rather than a 'neutral fantasy race.'

Starting rule:
- no fixed stat modifier;
- 21 free allocation points.

Material tendency:
- Primary: none;
- Secondary: none;
- broadly Neutral across ordinary material families.

Human-specific humor should often come from invisible default status: forms, furniture, scanners, biometric systems, and policy assumptions work for her without announcing that they were designed around her.

## 6. Elf

Starting modifiers:
- CHR +1
- INT +1
- STR -1

Material tendencies:
- Primary: Crystal, Wood
- Secondary: Glass, fine Stone/Marble, some Temporal
- Neutral: Ceramic, Synthetic, Precious Metal
- Uncommon: heavy industrial Metal, crude Concrete

Elf Wood is not automatically rustic.

Preferred Elf wood imagery:
- polished hardwood;
- carved or reworked bodies;
- arboreal grain;
- lacquer;
- sculptural preservation;
- museum-quality woodwork;
- living-tree visual references without requiring literal plant biology.

An Elf can still become industrial metal or concrete; it should simply be less common without strong competing events/talents.

## 7. Dwarf

Starting modifiers:
- STR +2
- CHR -1

Material tendencies:
- Primary: Stone, industrial Metal
- Secondary: Bronze, Concrete, architectural composites, some industrial Synthetic
- Neutral: Ceramic, Wood, Precious Metal
- Uncommon: Glass and delicate Crystal

Dwarf content should emphasize durability, craft, structural/industrial contexts, and mismatches with Human-scale infrastructure.

Avoid reducing every Dwarf event to mining or smithing. Modern Dwarves can be accountants, researchers, doctors, teachers, office workers, artists, or anything else.

## 8. Winged Kin

Formal English: Winged Kin  
Traditional Chinese: 翼族

Everyday shorthand:
- Angel
- 天使

The shorthand is culturally common and scientifically inaccurate.

Starting modifiers:
- CHR +1
- SPR +1
- STR -1

Material tendencies:
- Primary: Marble / pale or fine Stone
- Secondary: Crystal, Glass, Temporal
- Neutral: Wood, Ceramic, Synthetic, Precious Metal
- Uncommon: crude industrial materials

Wings vary in color, size, and functionality. Some individuals fly well; some poorly; some not at all.

Recurring social hooks:
- wing-compatible furniture that fits nobody;
- symbolic use in memorials/religion;
- workplace safety rules;
- transit accommodation;
- visual objectification;
- forms that ask whether wings are 'decorative.'

## 9. Demonkin

Formal English: Demonkin  
Traditional Chinese: 魔裔

Everyday shorthand:
- Demon
- 惡魔

Starting modifiers:
- CHR +2
- SPR -1

Material tendencies:
- Primary: Obsidian / dark Glass
- Secondary: Precious Metal, magical/sealed materials, Temporal
- Neutral: Stone, Synthetic, Ceramic, Crystal
- Uncommon: natural Wood

Horns, tails, coloration, and related traits are morphological rather than moral.

Recurring social hooks:
- presumed danger;
- scanner/security false positives;
- ceremonial symbolism;
- religious appropriation;
- contracts and bureaucracy treating appearance as evidence.

## 10. Dragonkin

Formal English: Dragonkin  
Traditional Chinese formal: 龍人族  
Traditional Chinese colloquial: 龍人

Do not use 龍裔 as the canonical zh-TW species label; it carries unwanted ambiguity with other fantasy properties/usage.

Starting modifiers:
- STR +1
- MNY +1
- SPR -1

Material tendencies:
- Primary: Precious Metal, Gemstone
- Secondary: Bronze, volcanic Stone, Crystal
- Neutral: ordinary Stone, Synthetic, Glass, Ceramic
- Uncommon: Wood

Dragonkin are broadly Human-sized humanoids with draconic features such as scales, horns, tails, and variable heat tolerance.

Recurring social hooks:
- future-form valuation;
- collateral assumptions;
- insurance pricing;
- precious-material expectations;
- thermal scanners;
- people conflating biological traits with financial worth.

## 11. Species-dependent event density

Ordinary life remains dominant.

A useful authoring target is that roughly 10–15% of events have strongly species-dependent text, conditions, or outcomes, while many more events may contain subtle species variants.

Species should be perceptible without turning the game into six separate campaigns.

## 12. Species and routes

Species may:
- influence material family tendencies;
- unlock or redirect specific event variants;
- affect institutional treatment;
- affect valuation, accommodation, or symbolism;
- interact with talents.

Species normally does **not**:
- force a social route;
- select a final material;
- create a unique ending tree for every species.

A shared ending should normally use species-specific prose/variants rather than duplicate Ending IDs.

## 13. True species vs registered species

`SPECIES` means true biological species.

`RSPECIES` means registered/institutional species.

Normally they match.

`Wrong Species Certificate` may cause them to differ. Biological material tendencies continue using true species, while institutions may use registered species where authored.

`Unregistered Species` may cause institutions to treat species data as unavailable.

## 14. Core principle

Species should answer:

> What kind of body and social assumptions did she begin life with?

It should not answer:

> What material must she become?
